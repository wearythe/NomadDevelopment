Rules & Guidelines

By commissioning me, you agree to the following terms. Please review them carefully before placing an order.

Respect my time & workflow

Commissioning me hires my technical expertise, not my full schedule. I operate as an independent developer with personal projects, ongoing client commitments, and dedicated personal time.

01

Avoid unnecessary pressure or rushing

Please refrain from repeatedly requesting updates or asking me to rush. Development progresses efficiently when planned according to an agreed timeline.

02

Schedule & availability boundaries

I do not handle commission requests or work during designated personal time, breaks, or off-hours. All inquiries sent during these hours will be answered when I am back at my desk.

03

Zero tolerance for disrespect

Hostility, condescension, threats, or aggressive behavior will not be tolerated under any circumstances. Clear, professional communication is required from both sides.

04

Professional communication

Questions, revisions, and feedback are always welcome and encouraged. However, demands, micromanagement, or rude behavior will not be accommodated.

05

Deadline & milestone management

When a milestone or deadline is agreed upon, I manage my timeline strictly to meet it. Constant reminders are unnecessary; if any unexpected delay arises, I will communicate it proactively.

06

Right of refusal and termination

I reserve the right to decline any proposal or terminate an active commission if a client exhibits abusive behavior, repeated disrespect, or breaches these guidelines.
